Frequently Asked Questions

How many South Africans live in the UAE?

An estimated 45,000–50,000 South Africans live in the UAE. The majority are in Dubai, with a significant community in Abu Dhabi. South Africans are one of the top five most active expat nationalities at Dubai Chamber — 873 active SA-owned companies were registered as of March 2026.

Do South Africans need a visa to enter UAE?

South Africans on a South African passport receive a 30-day visa on arrival in the UAE. For longer stays, you'll need a residence visa — employment, investor, freelance or golden visa. See our Moving to UAE guide for full details.

Do I still pay SARS tax if I live in UAE?

South Africa taxes residents on worldwide income. If you spend 183+ days per year outside SA (with 60+ consecutive days), you may qualify for a foreign income exemption under Section 10(1)(o)(ii). The first R1.25 million (2025 threshold) of foreign income is exempt. See our Money & Banking guide.

Are there South African schools in Dubai?

Yes. South African School Dubai (SASD) in Al Quoz offers the full CAPS (Curriculum Assessment Policy Statement) curriculum from Grade 1 through matric. See our Family & Schools guide.
