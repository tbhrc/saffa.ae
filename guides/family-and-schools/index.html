Family & Schools in Dubai

✓ SA-verified facts Updated August 2026 12 min read

Dubai has a well-developed education system with over 200 private schools covering more than 15 curricula — including South African CAPS. As a parent, your biggest decisions are: which curriculum, how much you can afford in fees, and which area to live in relative to the school.

SA (CAPS) Curriculum Schools in Dubai

🏫 South African School Dubai (SASD) The flagship CAPS school in Dubai. Located in Al Quoz, it follows the South African National Curriculum Assessment Policy Statement (CAPS) from Grade 1 through Grade 12 (Matric). Matric results are administered through the Western Cape Education Department, making them fully recognised in SA and internationally.
  • Location: Al Quoz, Dubai
  • Grades: Grade 1–12
  • Phone: +971-4-340-0608
  • Languages: English (Afrikaans available for some subjects)
  • Fees: Approx. AED 25,000–40,000/year (verify directly)

Alternative Curricula SA Families Choose

Many SA families settle on British curriculum (GCSE/A-Level) schools which are widely accepted for SA university applications if the right subjects are taken. IB (International Baccalaureate) is also popular for older students.

  • British curriculum (GCSE/A-Level) — Most common choice for SA expat families. KHDA-rated schools include Jumeirah English Speaking School, Raffles International, Dubai College and many more.
  • American curriculum — Credits are easily transferable; SA universities accept it with equivalent subjects.
  • IB (International Baccalaureate) — Premium option, globally recognised. Dubai has many IB World Schools.

School Fees in Dubai — What to Expect

Dubai has strict fee regulations through KHDA (Knowledge and Human Development Authority) and fees must be approved annually. All schools publish their approved fee schedule.

💰 Typical annual school fees (2025/2026)
  • Foundation/FS1–2 (nursery): AED 18,000–45,000/year
  • Primary (Grades 1–6): AED 22,000–65,000/year
  • Secondary (Grades 7–12): AED 30,000–80,000/year
  • Transport (school bus): AED 6,000–12,000/year additional
Fees vary significantly by school rating (Outstanding vs. Good vs. Acceptable per KHDA). Most schools also charge a one-off registration fee (AED 1,000–5,000).
⚠️ School places fill fast In Dubai's popular schools, waiting lists can be 6–18 months. Apply as soon as you have a confirmed move date — even before you arrive. Many schools allow applications with only a passport, before you have an Emirates ID.

Dependent (Family) Visa

To bring your spouse and children to the UAE, you need to sponsor their residence visa. This is called a Dependent Visa.

Requirements to sponsor dependents:

  • Your own valid UAE residence visa
  • Minimum monthly salary of AED 4,000 (to sponsor a spouse); some banks require AED 10,000+
  • Suitable accommodation (tenancy agreement / Ejari)
  • Attested and apostilled marriage certificate (for spouse)
  • Unabridged birth certificates for children
  • Medical fitness tests for all applicants (age-dependent)
✓ Tip for SA parents Get your children's unabridged birth certificates from SA Home Affairs before you leave SA — they take 6–12 weeks and are very difficult to process remotely. The Dubai Consulate cannot issue them, only authenticate them. See our Consulate guide.

Healthcare for Children

  • Paediatrics: Dubai has world-class paediatric care. Most large hospitals (Mediclinic, Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi, American Hospital) have dedicated paediatric departments.
  • Health insurance: Children must be covered under health insurance. Add them to your employer plan or take a separate family plan.
  • Vaccinations: Follow the UAE immunisation schedule (different from SA's). School enrolment requires proof of specific vaccines — check the KHDA requirements for your school.
  • Pharmacies: Aster, Life and Boots pharmacies are everywhere. Many SA medications (generics) are available; some brand names differ.

Nurseries and Childcare

For children under school age, Dubai has hundreds of nurseries licensed by KHDA. Popular SA expat choices:

  • Kids Kingdom Nursery (multiple locations)
  • Jumeirah International Nurseries
  • Raffles Nursery
  • Yellow Brick Road Nursery

Nursery hours in Dubai typically run 07:30–13:00 or 07:30–17:00. Fees: AED 1,500–4,000/month. Ask the SA community WhatsApp groups for recommendations near your area.
